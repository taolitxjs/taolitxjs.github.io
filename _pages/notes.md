I am currently seeking highly self-motivated Ph.D. students to join my research projects in the fields of security, machine learning, and mobile sensing. If students have prior experience in deep learning or signal processing, it would be a significant advantage. Interested students are encouraged to send their CVs, transcripts (both undergraduate and graduate, if applicable), TOEFL/IELTS scores, and any other materials they believe would be helpful to litao@purdue.edu. For more information, please visit our [Ph.D. program in Computer and Information Technology](https://polytechnic.purdue.edu/academics/phd-computer-and-information-technology). Please contact me only if you meet the [English proficiency requirements](https://www.purdue.edu/academics/ogsps/admissions/admission-requirements/), including both the overall score and all required subscores.

Due to the high volume of emails, I may NOT respond to generic inquiries lacking specific details.

I will respond if (1) I find your background relevant to my research, (2) your application materials are complete, and (3) you meet Purdue’s English proficiency requirements for graduate students.
